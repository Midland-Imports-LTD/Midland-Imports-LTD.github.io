About these colours

Many countries define their flag colours in Pantone, RAL or CMYK rather than hex, and some don't define exact shades at all. The hex codes here are the screen values most widely used for each flag, based on official specifications where they exist. For printed or sewn flags, match to the Pantone reference rather than the hex code, as colours shift between screen, paper and fabric.

Sources: Flags of the World (crwflags.com/fotw), The Flag Institute (flaginstitute.org), and each country's official flag specification where published.
